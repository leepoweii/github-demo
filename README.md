# A Nice Day

The weather is nice today. The sky is clear and blue, with only a few thin clouds drifting by, and the sun is warm without being harsh. A light breeze moves through the trees, carrying the faint smell of grass and coffee from the café down the street.

On days like this, it is hard to stay indoors. People linger a little longer on their walks, neighbors stop to chat, and even the usual rush of the city seems to slow down. It is the kind of afternoon that makes small things feel worth noticing.

Tomorrow may bring rain, but for now, the day is simply good.
